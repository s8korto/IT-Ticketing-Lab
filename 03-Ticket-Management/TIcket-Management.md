# Ticket Management

## Objective

Demonstrate the end-to-end management of IT support requests using osTicket, including ticket creation, prioritisation, assignment, troubleshooting, and resolution.

---

## Business Scenario

X company has begun using osTicket as its central IT service desk. Employees submit support requests for common technical issues, which are then reviewed, prioritised, and assigned to the appropriate support team.

Three realistic incidents were created to demonstrate different areas of the service desk.
