# Creating Users and Agents

## Objective 

After installing osTicket, the next step is to configure the help desk environment by creating departments, roles, teams, support agents, and end users. This ensures support requests are routed to the appropriate technicians while maintaining proper access control.

---

## Business Scenario 

X Company is a medium-sized company with approximately **180 employees** across multiple departments, including Human Resources, Finance, Sales, and Warehouse Operations.

The company recently implemented **osTicket** to replace support requests sent directly through email. Before the system can be used by employees, the IT Administrator must configure the help desk structure.

---

## Business Requirement

- Creating departments
- Creating support teams
- Configuring user roles and permissions
- Creating support agents
- Creating end users
- Preparing the system for ticket routing
